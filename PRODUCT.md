# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Individuals who want one understandable financial picture across accounts, bills, income, goals, and optional work-money needs. They use the product to decide what is safe now, understand what may change next, and test choices before acting.

## Product Purpose

The Ledger turns fragmented financial records into auditable decisions. Success means a user can understand the current picture, inspect the assumptions behind a forecast, compare a hypothetical with reality, and involve an AI without surrendering execution authority.

## Brand Personality

Independent, evidence-first, and quietly exact. The product should create calm through visible reasoning rather than reassurance, and convey technical depth without asking ordinary users to understand implementation details.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic bank dashboards, sales-led fintech funnels, game-first money apps, opaque AI chat shells, invented metrics, and interfaces that hide consequential assumptions behind a single confident number.

## Design Principles

- Show the answer with its evidence close enough to inspect.
- Treat uncertainty as part of the result, not as fine print.
- Keep the model useful while keeping human authority explicit.
- Let one coherent ledger serve every interface and integration.
- Make advanced and optional layers discoverable without making them the product identity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard operation. Preserve visible focus, semantic controls, touch-friendly targets, reduced-motion alternatives, and redundant status cues that do not rely on color alone. Financial language should stay factual, non-shaming, and readable without specialist knowledge.
