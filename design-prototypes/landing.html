The Instruments

Every figure, engraved and accountable.

Not another dashboard of charts you ignore. Each tool is a working instrument — it does one thing, shows its reasoning, and never moves real money without your stamp.

No. 01

The Sea-Chart

Your cash-flow forecast as a coastline. Drag the line to model a what-if and watch your overdraft probability re-sound in real time. Probabilistic, not a flat guess.

No. 02

The Clerk

An assistant that drafts the move — re-budget, schedule, reconcile — then hands you a diff plate to authorize. It proposes; you stamp. Nothing happens behind your back.

No. 03

Minted Goals

Fund a goal and strike a coin for the cabinet. Saving becomes a collection of specimens you actually want to complete — the only game your money should play.

No. 04

Import Anything

Messy statements, mystery CSVs, duplicate rows. The Detangler reads them, reconciles them, and hands back a clean register — no manual category roulette.

No. 05

Nightly Auditor

While you sleep, it reconciles the day, flags the odd charge, and leaves a short note in the margin. You wake up to an account already balanced.

No. 06

Tax, Filed Quietly

1099s, deductions, quarterly estimates — tracked all year and stamped into a packet at season's end. The dread becomes a form you just sign.
