## Concept

**The Mint Floor** — A live intaglio printing-press where you press REAL payments into the Sapphire Card debt boss ($842.13) and watch the engraving plate physically thicken with every strike — Off is the printed certificate at rest (no canvas), Ambient is the page breathing under candlelight (wet-ink re-engraving, no swarm), Full is the running mint floor where cursor-wake, scroll, and hold-to-strike herd a GPGPU guilloche ink field and stamp coins whose faces show the real remaining balance.

### OFF mode
Byte-for-byte the plain ledger: the WebGL chunk is NEVER imported in Off (MintCanvasLazy is not mounted; verified by `document.querySelector('.mint-floor canvas')===null` and by the absence of the MintFieldScene chunk in the network panel). A single static engraved SVG plate renders the debt boss as an intaglio banknote vignette: the boss serial = the REAL remaining balance $842.13 (Sapphire Credit Card, the true seed liability — NOT the fabricated $4,696/$5,000 the prior concept used), a hairline guilloche border, five engraved tick-marks along the bottom edge marking the 20% phase bands (none cleared at $842.13), and one quiet real `<button>` Strike control (≥44px, focusable, native focus ring). Money figures use the semantic credit/debit/gold .figure class; zero phosphor, zero indigo, no motion beyond native focus rings. Strike STILL works in Off (instant, no juice): hp ticks down by the dialed payment, ticks fill, plate-peel-equivalent (a static layer swap, no animation) on a phase break. Cold-poster acceptance: a screenshot reads "engraved mint certificate" and must FAIL a "neon / arcade / 8-bit / navy-gold debt game" reflex check before any game chrome exists. Off is the default for unknown/weak GPUs and for reduced-motion users who push the dial past Ambient (Full clamps to Ambient under reduced-motion).

### AMBIENT mode
The page breathing under candlelight. A low-DPR (cap 1.25) single-pass canvas mounts behind the same engraved plate — NO FBO, NO ping-pong, NO bloom, NO swarm, NO shake, NO audio, chrome stays parchment. The signature gesture Ambient OWNS (and neither Off nor Full repeats identically) is WET-INK RE-ENGRAVING: the guilloche border lines are redrawn by a slow traveling stroke-dashoffset (~18s linear loop) as if a pen is retracing them, and the boss serial numeral's strokes shimmer one glyph at a time (single-glyph serial retrace). Plus a ≤3° cursor-parallax plate tilt (rAF-lerped, not framer) and a CSS candle-flicker on the gold leaf. Curl drift sits at its floor (uDrift=0.04). M1 PROOF (hard gate, built FIRST per mustFix): capture Ambient and a temporarily-dimmed Full side-by-side at a 1m-equivalent crop; Ambient must read "breathing but calm / wet-ink retracing", visibly NOT "Full turned down". The distinguishing tell is gestural, not brightness: Ambient has the traveling pen-retrace and zero flecks; dimmed Full still shows a drifting fleck swarm + cursor wake. If it fails the 1m eyeball, Ambient gets a more categorically distinct gesture before any Full art ships.

### FULL mode

### Interaction loop
CHARGE (anticipation — the longest, most-telegraphed phase since dopamine peaks on anticipation, not payoff: die descends, field is drawn in, live readout "paying $X · you're $Y from a CRIT" where Y=max(0, $43.75−X)) → RELEASE (ONE dominant THUNK + amplitude-mapped shake; everything else staggered 8–24ms behind so one strike reads as one satisfying beat) → READ (HP serial ticks down by the REAL payment; a coin stamps that exact figure; combo tier rises; on a 20% band the plate PEELS to the next phase's deeper engraving) → REPEAT, escalating. Each consecutive on-beat strike (within 1100ms) visibly upgrades the floor — more sparks, brighter phosphor, faster ink — so you want strike #2 and #3 before the boss is near dead. The CRIT teaser makes the next strike feel within reach (variable magnitude legible pre-strike); CRIT being a REAL threshold the player crosses by holding longer gives honest slot-machine pull with no RNG. Honesty guardrail: celebrations fire ONLY for the backend JUICE_EVENTS allowlist (BossDamagedCrit, BossPhaseBroken, BossDefeated, BossSealed, LevelUp, ...); a plain strike acknowledges quietly; near-misses are NEVER celebrated (telegraph "$12 from a CRIT" during charge = ethical anticipation; a near-CRIT strike resolves silently).

### Charge → magnitude → CRIT mapping
EXACT mapping (continuous, never stepped — stepped charge would kill the deliberate-dial skill): on the rAF charge loop, charge advances 0→1 over hold time, charge += dt/CHARGE_TO_MAX_MS clamped to 1.0, with CHARGE_TO_MAX_MS = 1400ms (a full-power $120 strike takes a 1.4s hold). payment_cents = round(lerp(minPayment, MAX_STRIKE, easeOutCubic(charge))) where minPayment=3_500 ($35.00, the real Sapphire minimum), MAX_STRIKE=12_000 ($120, a real allowed payment for an $842 card), easeOutCubic(c)=1−(1−c)^3. Because easeOutCubic is fast-early/decelerating, the dollar figure moves a lot in the first ~300ms then fine-tunes near the top — giving precise dial control right around the CRIT line. PAYMENT→CRIT: CRIT fires when payment > $43.75. Solving the lerp: $43.75 is reached at charge where lerp=4_375 → easeOutCubic(charge)=(4375−3500)/(12000−3500)=0.1029 → charge≈0.0353 (~50ms of hold past the tap floor). So a quick tap (<~50ms hold) yields ≤$43.75 = NO CRIT (the 'grey/quick' zone); any deliberate hold past ~50ms crosses CRIT. HOW A FIRST-TIMER DELIBERATELY LANDS A CRIT: the live readout shows "paying $X · you're $Y from a CRIT" (Y=max(0,$43.75−X)); the charge gauge renders a fixed CRIT-LINE MARKER at the $43.75 position that the rising fill climbs toward; when the fill crosses, the readout flips to "CRIT READY" BEFORE release — so the player watches the fill approach the marked line (anticipation = the dopamine driver) and releases past it on purpose. HONESTY: the dialed payment_cents is dispatched verbatim as the STRIKE action's amount, applied as raw-amount damage AND posted as the PaymentPosted-equivalent (XP+20); CRIT is evaluated as amount>$43.75 on that exact same number — so "$12 from a CRIT" is a genuinely achievable, player-moveable goal each strike, and the posted number is a real PaymentPosted (never inflated by CRIT). No overcharge penalty: the top of the range is just MAX_STRIKE clamped, never a backfire.

### Combo / "beat" spec
The 'beat' = COMBO_WINDOW_MS = 1100ms. CADENCE CUE: a faint stroke-dashoffset ring around the Strike control pulses on a 1100ms period (one full dash sweep per beat) — the visible metronome; in Full it is reinforced by the die-thunk audio landing on the beat and a subtle pitch tick. A strike RELEASED within 1100ms of the previous strike's release increments combo (combo++); a release after the window resets combo to 1. HEAT TIER: heat rises by tier as combo climbs, capped at MONTHLY_HEAT_CAP=6; tier boundaries at combo = 1,2,3,5,7,10 → HEAT 1..6 (escalating spacing so later tiers are earned). ESCALATION CADENCE: the floor visibly upgrades every strike at low combo and every tier crossing thereafter — concretely, each HEAT tier raises (a) particle density ~+15%/tier (capped by FrameBudget), (b) phosphor bloom intensity (uCombo), and (c) audio pitch +1 semitone/tier. A CRIT advances HEAT FASTER (the honest non-cosmetic payoff): a CRIT strike counts as +2 toward the next tier boundary instead of +1, so chasing CRITs compounds the escalation loop while the real damage stays raw-amount. IDLE DECAY: TICK_COMBO_DECAY runs on a timer — after 1100ms with no strike, combo and HEAT cool one tier every 900ms toward embers (a slow ease, never a snap); letting it cool to HEAT 0 is the honest in-session loss condition. All timings (1100/1400/900ms) are constants in gameData.ts, tunable against the perf trace in Phase 4.

### Keyboard-reactive Full
Keyboard/non-pointer users get a genuinely REACTIVE Full (field aim + charge + strike), not just the verb. FIELD AIM: arrow keys (and WASD) write a synthetic velocity impulse into the SAME RG velocity-wake texture the cursor stamps — each arrow keydown adds a fixed-magnitude velocity vector in that direction at a focus-driven aim point, decaying with the same ×0.95/frame fade, so a keyboard user genuinely herds the ink field with persistent-wake feel. CHARGE+STRIKE: the Strike control is a real ≥44px `<button>`; we attach OWN keydown/keyup handlers (NOT relying on the native click) — on keydown for Space we call preventDefault() (stop page scroll) and IGNORE auto-repeat via `if (e.repeat) return;`, start the rAF charge ramp; on keyup we dispatch STRIKE with the final charge. We SUPPRESS the implicit native click (which would fire a second strike on Space keyup / Enter keydown) by calling preventDefault on the click or flagging the keyboard-handled release, so there is exactly ONE strike per press. Enter is handled the same via keydown-charge/keyup-strike for consistency. setPointerCapture is the pointer analog; for keyboard the focus owns the lifecycle so there is no stuck-charge. ARIA: per-frame charge is NOT announced (noise); only the RESOLVED payment + CRIT result is announced via aria-live='polite' after release. Tab order: mode dial → Strike → mute. Tested against NVDA/JAWS behavior (they fire Space on keydown with repeat) by relying on the button's keydown/keyup lifecycle, not on reading event.key.

### Real-mechanic bindings

### Motion spec
REBALANCED CHARGE MAPPING (verifier#1 mustFix - the old easeOutCubic put CRIT at ~50ms = effectively binary; this widens the deliberate sub-CRIT zone and moves the CRIT line to a telegraphed mid-hold point):
- TAP-vs-HOLD DISCRIMINATION (new, mustFix): TAP_MAX_MS = 140ms. A pointerdown/keydown released within 140ms is a TAP => payment forced to a fixed sub-CRIT 'quick strike' = MIN_CENTS=3500 ($35), isCrit=false, NO charge ramp consulted. A press held >140ms enters CHARGE mode (the rAF ramp starts only after the 140ms tap window elapses, so the ramp clock begins at t=140ms). This guarantees an instant tap is always a clean, intentional non-CRIT, distinct from the start of a hold, on BOTH pointer and keyboard.
- CHARGE RAMP (only for holds >140ms): charge advances 0->1 over CHARGE_TO_MAX_MS = 1600ms; charge += dt/1600 clamped to 1.0. payment_cents = round(lerp(MIN_CENTS, MAX_STRIKE, easeInOutCubic(charge))) where easeInOutCubic gives a gentle start (so the dollar figure does NOT leap past CRIT instantly) then accelerates - the opposite shaping of the old fast-early curve, which is what created the binary problem.
- CRIT LINE position: CRIT fires when payment > 4375. Solve lerp=4375 => easeInOutCubic(charge) = (4375-3500)/(12000-3500) = 0.1029 => for easeInOutCubic the inverse gives charge ~= 0.29 (well into the hold, ~about 0.29*1600 = ~465ms of charge i.e. ~605ms total press incl tap window). RESULT: a quick tap (<=140ms) = $35 guaranteed sub-CRIT; a short-but-deliberate hold up to ~600ms stays sub-CRIT and lets the player DIAL a value between $35 and ~$44; crossing the marked CRIT line at ~29% charge is a clearly telegraphed, deliberate act => the three claimed outcomes (tap / dialed-sub-CRIT / hold-past-CRIT) are all reachable on purpose. (All constants live in gameData.ts and are re-tuned against the Phase-4 perf trace; the EXACT easing inverse is computed numerically in code, not hand-waved.)
- CRIT-LINE MARKER: the charge gauge renders a fixed tick at the screen position corresponding to charge~=0.29; the rising fill climbs toward it; readout shows 'paying $X . you are $Y from a CRIT' (Y=max(0,4375-payment)/100); on crossing, readout flips to 'CRIT READY' BEFORE release.

STRIKE JUICE TIMING (one strike = one beat): die-thunk at t=0; squash-overshoot scaleY [1,0.86,1.04,1] over 180ms staggered +8ms; shockwave opacity/scale sin [0,1,0] +16ms; ink-resettle + combo tick +24ms; bloom pulse +24ms. Screenshake: A=clamp(payment/12000*8px,0,8px), A*e^(-t/tau), tau~40ms, total <180ms, transform-only.
COMBO/CADENCE: COMBO_WINDOW_MS=1100 (the beat). Cadence ring = stroke-dashoffset full sweep per 1100ms around StrikeControl. Release within 1100ms of prior release => combo++ (integer); release after => combo=1. HEAT: SEPARATE heatProgress accumulator (verifier#3 mustFix) advances +1 per on-beat strike, +2 on CRIT; TIER_BOUNDARIES=[1,2,3,5,7,10] map heatProgress to heatTier 1..6 (cap 6). combo stays a pure integer; heatProgress is the only thing CRIT's +2 touches. CRIT moment: MintHUD HEAT meter visibly JUMPS two notches with a distinct flash so the +2 payoff is PERCEPTIBLE (verifier#2 mustFix). IDLE DECAY: after 1100ms idle, cool one tier every HEAT_DECAY_MS=900ms (slow ease toward embers), HEAT 0 = honest in-session loss.
CHARGE ANTICIPATION: uCharge ramps power3.out as die descends + ink sucked inward; die-descent speed, ink-suck intensity, faint pitch-rising hum ESCALATE as fill nears the CRIT marker.
REGISTRATION SNAP: framer spring {stiffness:520, damping:16} (one crisp overshoot) on crosshairs; the snap === theme-swap clip-path inset() curtain 420ms ease-out-expo. PLATE-PEEL on BossPhaseBroken only: clip-path/mask wipe 520ms ease-out-expo.
